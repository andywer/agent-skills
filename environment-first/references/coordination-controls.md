# Conditional coordination controls

Read this reference only when the task has an irreplaceable result surface, an evidence-class prerequisite, asynchronous or dependent delegation, protected promotion, or a real need for comparative or joinable execution evidence. These controls are not a default operating mode.

## Probe the real boundary

Before relying on a runtime, interface, permission, isolation claim, mutable input, or promotion path, exercise the smallest safe real unit. Distinguish the working directory or sandbox from inherited instructions, credentials, plugins, caches, configuration, and state stores. Reuse an existing valid environment or lineage after checking identity, freshness, and scope.

Record a preflight fact only when another context or later decision must rely on it. Do not create a general receipt for facts consumed immediately.

## Protect result surfaces transactionally

Treat accepted and irreplaceable artifacts as protected. Write a candidate to a separate or versioned path, validate it, then promote it. Before an unavoidable in-place transformation of irreplaceable data, create and verify a rollback copy.

A successful command does not prove that its output is safe to replace the prior result. A checksum can detect drift; it cannot establish semantic completeness.

## Gate evidence promotion

When downstream work depends on provenance or a particular evidence class, declare that requirement in task-local language before promotion. Compare the observed evidence with the requirement. A replay, summary, receipt, or derived artifact cannot silently satisfy a stronger live, direct, or independently attested requirement.

On mismatch, absence, or ambiguity, stop the dependent action or promotion until the evidence arrives or an authorized decision changes the requirement. Use identifiers and evidence fields the actual harness exposes; do not invent a universal enum. For consequential external effects, task-native evidence from the affected system establishes what occurred; agent prose, status, and terminal returns support coordination but do not establish the effect.

## Join asynchronous or comparative work only when necessary

Use the lightest append-only dispatch, completion, and artifact-lineage record that makes a real join reconstructible. Instrument every condition in a comparison. Do not call dispatch and spawn atomic unless the harness couples them transactionally.

Distinguish a retry from a duplicate only when that distinction matters downstream. Preserve `unknown`, `unpaired`, or equivalent states when a child, receipt, terminal result, or required field cannot be joined. Use verified polling and artifact inspection when the harness has no join facility; do not infer completion from silence. Keep observation and cancellation distinct: observation informs a decision without changing the work, while cancellation is an explicit authorized stop.

Accept a result only after the material unit identity, evidence, completion, protected-boundary, and artifact-lineage checks pass. Require no field that the claim does not consume. If a missing field prevents the required provenance or safety conclusion, fail closed; otherwise label it unavailable and continue.

Keep detailed events off the top agent's orientation. The top agent needs the joined decision and evidence pointer, not the control-plane transcript.

## Remove the control

Stop maintaining receipts, joins, or protected candidates when their triggering hazard ends. Do not keep the full machinery active for later direct or locally verifiable work.
