# Complex Work

Keep the work aligned with the user's requirements, intended outcome and underlying purpose as context, evidence and execution conditions change. Start with useful work and maintain only the understanding and protections needed to continue reliably. A long or complex task can still be handled directly.

## Make useful progress

Reach the natural result or a coherent experiment early. Choose work large enough to change the answer; narrow it when an unproven interface, concrete hazard or observed failure calls for diagnosis. Inspect the tools, permissions, inputs, mutable state and result surfaces the work will actually touch.

Count progress in required outcomes, decision-changing evidence or a defensible blocker. Scaffolding contributes when the result path consumes it or it changes a decision. Add or retain a component, rule, helper, format or process step only when the objective cannot reasonably be served more simply. Remove controls when their triggering need ends.

When repairs repeat without useful evidence, revisit the objective and choose whether to run a safe informative slice, use a proven mechanism, narrow the claim or return the blocker. Further repair earns its place when the defect prevents execution, crosses a hard boundary, irreversibly contaminates evidence or makes the observation uninterpretable.

## Preserve the orientation needed to continue

When work may outlive a comfortable context or cross a handoff, keep a compact, revisable account of the governing objective, underlying intent, material requirements, relevant explored approaches, established results, remaining work and next useful action. Distinguish accepted results from proposals and blocked or deferred work. Point to sources where another context needs the basis for a decision; these are content needs, not required headings.

Keep detailed evidence and history on their natural surfaces. Persist an orientation only when later work needs it; use [game-plan.md](references/game-plan.md) for a durable account. If it is stale, recover the relevant facts from governing instructions, primary sources and accepted artifacts before relying on it.

When the user changes the objective or acceptance conditions, reopen only prior conclusions that depended on the change. Reorient when work expands sideways or the user has to correct the operating approach.

## Protect the boundaries before acting

Preserve user control, permissions, people, secrets, protected data and irreversible external state. Protect accepted and irreplaceable artifacts: write candidates separately, validate them, then promote deliberately. Before unavoidable in-place transformation of irreplaceable data, create and verify a rollback copy. Read [coordination-controls.md](references/coordination-controls.md) before protected promotion, provenance-dependent work or a consequential asynchronous join needs its mechanics.

Distinguish irreversible evidence contamination from a reversible confidence limitation. The former may require protection before observing an outcome; the latter usually calls for a narrower claim, an invalidation condition or a cheap probe. Acceptance-critical reproducibility still belongs before the run it protects.

Before a protected, held-out, scarce or acceptance-bearing run, check whether the execution path is adequately established. If it is unproven or materially changed, send a harmless specimen through the relevant path first. Reuse adequate prior evidence after checking identity, freshness and scope. A surrogate supports only the boundary it exercised; name any material live boundary that remains untested.

Bound material spending, unsafe retries and consequential mutations before running, including atomic one-shot operations. Read [execution-leases.md](references/execution-leases.md) when those risks apply. Ordinary enforceable caps and stop conditions may suffice; renewal, receipts and accounting are conditional on a continuation or coordination need, not repetition alone.

## Verify the claim

Use the cheapest credible observation that could expose a failure of the required outcome. Prefer deterministic checks for mechanical claims and independent judgment where unresolved meaning or integration could change a consequential decision. Reuse checks until a change could affect the claim they support.

A successful command, receipt, lifecycle status or agent report does not establish semantic correctness or consequential external effects. Verify those effects on the affected system. Keep evidence, inference and remaining uncertainty distinct.

## Delegate and intervene where useful

Delegate only when available, authorized and useful. Give a bounded assignment its purpose, scope, acceptance condition, boundaries, relevant sources and evidence to return. Keep integration and acceptance with the delegating agent. A dispatch message is sufficient unless a later handoff or promotion needs a durable brief.

When plausible final results could conceal materially different interpretations or methods, check the work early enough to redirect it. Use the smallest useful operational observation or question; lifecycle status and final artifacts answer different questions. Read [subagent-brief.md](references/subagent-brief.md) for consequential dispatch, supervision or partial-return mechanics, and [Codex defaults](harness-defaults/codex.md) only when using its coordination or session-inspection capabilities. Routine monitoring is unnecessary.

When a brief conflicts materially with observed reality or reasonable interpretations change scope, safety, acceptance or verification, hold the affected action and resolve the tension before inventing a workaround. Continue independent useful work. A worker needing an upstream decision uses a known suitable live channel or returns a safe partial for deliberate resumption; the reference supplies the portable fallback.

Keep the same worker for repair of an unchanged bounded unit; use fresh context when independence is the point. Never let a producer self-promote across a protected or consequential boundary or edit the independent verdict it is repairing against. Preserve task-assigned approval and review gates. Assess review findings against the current claim and consequence: repair, limit or invalidate a claim, defer optional work, or block a hard boundary as appropriate. A finding does not become a blocker merely because it came from review.

If operational drift remains hard to diagnose, a sparse, fresh read-only assessment may help; the Codex reference describes bounded session-based reflection. It adds no standing observer role.

## Deliver and close honestly

Compare the result and supporting evidence with the user's objective. Deliver the requested result, distinguishing complete work from material blocked, deferred or partial outcomes. Optional work does not keep the task open. Before closing a consequential assembled result, check for a major omission beyond the known checklist.

Use a fresh final reviewer only when consequential integration risk remains unsettled or the task requires one. Read [closeout.md](references/closeout.md) when complex assembly, stale orientation or a consequential handoff needs further guidance; using delegation or another control alone does not require another review.
